# ShopEase Exchange Policy

## Overview

ShopEase allows you to exchange most items within **30 days of delivery** for a different size or colour. Exchanges are processed as a simultaneous return of the original item and dispatch of the replacement — you do not need to pay for shipping in either direction.

## What Can Be Exchanged

You can request an exchange for:

- **Incorrect size**: The item you received fits differently than expected.
- **Incorrect colour**: You received a different colour than what you ordered.
- **Preference change**: You want to try a different size or colour variant of the same product.

Exchanges are subject to **stock availability**. If your desired size or colour is out of stock at the time of your request, you will be offered a full refund instead.

## What Cannot Be Exchanged

The following items are **not eligible** for exchange:

- Innerwear, lingerie, socks, and swimwear (hygiene reasons).
- Items marked "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" on the product page.
- Items that have been worn, washed, altered, or damaged after delivery.
- Items without original tags and packaging.
- Customised or personalised orders.

## How to Initiate an Exchange

1. Log in to your ShopEase account and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the relevant order and click **Exchange Item**.
3. Choose the item you want to exchange and select your reason.
4. Pick your **preferred size and/or colour** from the available options. You will see live stock availability.
5. Confirm the exchange. A pickup request will be automatically scheduled.

Our pickup partner will collect the original item from your address within **3–5 business days**. The replacement item will be dispatched once the original item is picked up and scanned by the courier.

## Exchange Delivery Timeline

- **Metro cities** (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad): Replacement dispatched within 1–2 business days of pickup. Delivered in 2–4 business days.
- **Other cities and towns**: Replacement dispatched within 2–3 business days. Delivered in 5–7 business days.

Total exchange turnaround: **6–10 business days** from the date of pickup.

## Price Difference in Exchanges

- **Same product, different size/colour**: No price difference. Exchange is free.
- **Different product exchange**: Not currently supported. Please return the original item and place a new order for the desired product.

## Exchange vs. Return: Which Should I Choose?

| Situation | Recommended Action |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Wrong size, same product available | Exchange |
| Don't like the product at all | Return |
| Want a completely different product | Return + New Order |
| Item damaged/wrong item sent | Report Issue (replacement dispatched immediately) |

## Multiple Exchanges

Each item can be exchanged **only once**. If the exchanged item also does not fit, you can initiate a **return** for a full refund, provided you are still within the 30-day window from the original delivery date.

## Exchanges for Gifts

If you received a ShopEase order as a gift, you can exchange the item using the **Order ID** (shown on the packing slip inside the parcel). You will need to provide your own address for pickup and redelivery. Refunds for gift orders are issued as **ShopEase Wallet credit** unless the original buyer requests an account refund.

## Damaged Items Received

If you received a damaged, defective, or wrong item, please do **not** raise an exchange request. Instead:

1. Go to **My Orders → Report an Issue** within **48 hours** of delivery.
2. Attach clear photos showing the damage or wrong item.
3. We will arrange a **free priority replacement** dispatched within 1–2 business days, or a full refund if the item is out of stock.
