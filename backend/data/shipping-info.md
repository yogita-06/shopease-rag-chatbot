# ShopEase Shipping Information

## Delivery Coverage

ShopEase delivers across **India** to over **26,000 pin codes**, including all major metro cities, Tier-2 cities, and a large number of Tier-3 towns and rural areas. You can check whether your pin code is serviceable on the product page or at checkout.

We currently do **not offer international shipping**. All orders are dispatched from our fulfilment centres located in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru.

## Shipping Options and Costs

### Standard Delivery
- **Timeline**: 5–7 business days from dispatch.
- **Cost**: Free on all orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 attract a flat shipping fee of ₹49.

### Express Delivery
- **Timeline**: 2–3 business days from dispatch.
- **Cost**: ₹99 flat, available in select metro cities and major Tier-2 towns.
- **Availability**: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Jaipur, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Surat.

### Same-Day Delivery
- **Timeline**: Delivered within the same calendar day if ordered before 11 AM IST.
- **Cost**: ₹149 flat.
- **Availability**: Currently limited to select areas within Mumbai (South, West, and Central zones) and Bengaluru (Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, Electronic City, Jayanagar).

## Dispatch Timelines

Orders placed before **2 PM IST on a business day** are typically dispatched on the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM or on public holidays are dispatched on the next business day. During sale events (Big Billion Day equivalents, End-of-Season Sales), dispatch may be delayed by 1–2 business days due to high volume.

## Tracking Your Shipment

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:

- An **SMS** with a tracking link sent to your registered mobile number.
- An **email** with the courier name, AWB (airway bill) number, and a direct tracking URL.
- Real-time updates in the **My Orders** section of your ShopEase account or app.

ShopEase partners with leading logistics providers including Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC, XpressBees, and Ekart for reliable last-mile delivery across India.

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available for orders **up to ₹5,000** and for pin codes supported by our COD logistics partners. COD availability is shown at checkout. Please keep the exact amount ready at the time of delivery to avoid delays.

## Undelivered and Returned-to-Origin (RTO) Orders

If a delivery attempt fails because no one is available to receive the package, the courier will make **up to 3 delivery attempts** on consecutive business days. After 3 failed attempts, the package is returned to ShopEase (RTO). In such cases:

- Prepaid orders: A refund is issued after the package reaches our fulfilment centre (7–14 business days).
- COD orders: No charge is levied. The order is simply cancelled.

To avoid RTO, ensure your delivery address and mobile number are correct when placing the order.

## Shipping During Festivals and Peak Season

During major Indian festivals — Diwali, Dussehra, Eid, Christmas, and New Year — and during large sale events, delivery timelines may be extended by **2–4 business days** due to high order volumes. We recommend ordering early during these periods.
