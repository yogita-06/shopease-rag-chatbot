How do I place an order on ShopEase?
To place an order, browse our catalogue and add items to your cart. Once you're ready, go to your cart and click "Proceed to Checkout". Enter your delivery address and choose a payment method to complete your order.

What payment methods does ShopEase accept?
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible pin codes.

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days. Express delivery (available in select cities) takes 2–3 business days. You will receive a tracking link via SMS and email once your order is shipped.

Can I track my order?
Yes. Visit the "My Orders" section in your account and click on your order to see real-time tracking updates. You can also track using the link sent to your registered mobile number.

What is ShopEase's return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy from the date of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags intact. Sale items are not eligible for return.

How do I initiate a return?
Go to "My Orders", select the item you want to return, and click "Return Item". Choose a reason and submit the request. Our pickup partner will collect the item within 3–5 business days.

When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. The amount will be credited to your original payment method or ShopEase wallet, as per your preference.

How do I find my correct size?
Each product page has a Size Guide button. Click it to see measurements in inches and centimetres. If you are between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a comfortable fit.

Can I exchange an item for a different size or colour?
Yes, exchanges are available within 30 days of delivery. Initiate an exchange from "My Orders". Please note that exchanges are subject to stock availability.

What should I do if I receive a damaged or wrong item?
We apologise for the inconvenience. Please raise a complaint within 48 hours of delivery via "My Orders" → "Report an Issue". Attach photos of the item and we will arrange a replacement or full refund promptly.

Does ShopEase ship outside India?
Currently ShopEase delivers only within India. We are working on expanding international shipping and will announce it on our website and social media.

Is Cash on Delivery available for all orders?
COD is available for orders below ₹5,000 and in pin codes serviced by our logistics partners. You can check COD availability at checkout by entering your pin code.

How do I apply a coupon or promo code?
At checkout, enter your coupon code in the "Apply Coupon" field and click "Apply". The discount will be reflected in your order total before payment. Only one coupon can be applied per order.

Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Go to "My Orders" and click "Cancel Order". If the order has already been shipped, you will need to initiate a return after delivery.

How do I contact ShopEase customer support?
You can reach us via live chat on our website (9 AM – 9 PM IST), email at support@shopease.in, or call our helpline at 1800-XXX-XXXX (toll-free, Mon–Sat 9 AM – 7 PM).
