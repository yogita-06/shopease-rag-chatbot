# ShopEase Customer Support — Contact Information

## Overview

ShopEase's customer support team is available across multiple channels to help you with orders, returns, exchanges, payments, and any other queries. We are committed to resolving your issue as quickly as possible.

## Support Channels

### Live Chat (Fastest Response)

Live chat is the **quickest way** to get help for most issues, including order status, return initiation, and payment failures.

- **Availability**: 9 AM – 9 PM IST, 7 days a week (including Sundays and most public holidays).
- **Where**: Click the **"Chat with Us"** button on the ShopEase website (bottom-right corner) or tap **"Help"** in the ShopEase mobile app.
- **Average wait time**: Under 2 minutes during regular hours. Up to 5–10 minutes during peak sale periods.

### Email Support

For detailed queries, documentation requests, or issues that require investigation (e.g., payment disputes, missing refunds, escalations), email is preferred.

- **Email address**: support@shopease.in
- **Availability**: Emails are monitored Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM IST.
- **Response time**: Within **24 hours** on business days. Up to **48 hours** during sale events.
- **What to include**: Your registered mobile number or email, Order ID (if applicable), and a clear description of the issue. Attach screenshots or photos for faster resolution.

### Phone Support (Helpline)

- **Toll-free number**: 1800-XXX-XXXX

## Self-Service Options (No Wait Required)

Many common tasks can be completed instantly without contacting support:

| Task | Where to do it |
|------|---------------|
| Track an order | My Orders → View Order |
| Initiate a return | My Orders → Return Item |
| Request an exchange | My Orders → Exchange Item |
| Cancel an order | My Orders → Cancel Order |
| Download invoice | My Orders → Invoice |
| Update address/phone | My Account → Profile |
| Check refund status | My Orders → Return Status |
| Apply wallet balance | Cart → Apply Wallet |

## Tips for Faster Resolution

1. Keep your **Order ID** ready before contacting support.
2. Use **Live Chat** for the fastest responses.
3. For refund queries, wait until the expected timeline has fully passed before escalating.
4. For payment failures, share your **bank transaction reference number (UTR/RRN)** for quicker tracing.
