# ShopEase Order Tracking

## How to Track Your Order

Once your order is dispatched from our fulfilment centre, ShopEase provides multiple ways to stay updated on your delivery.

### 1. Track via My Orders (Recommended)

1. Log in to your ShopEase account on the website or app.
2. Tap or click **My Orders** in the top-right menu.
3. Find the order you want to track and click **View Order Details**.
4. You will see a **real-time tracking timeline** showing:
   - Order confirmed
   - Order packed
   - Dispatched from warehouse
   - In transit (with current city/hub location)
   - Out for delivery
   - Delivered

The tracking page refreshes automatically every 15 minutes.

### 2. Track via SMS Link

When your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS on your registered mobile number containing:

- The **courier partner name** (e.g., Delhivery, Blue Dart, XpressBees, Ekart, DTDC).
- The **AWB (Airway Bill) number**.
- A **clickable tracking link** that opens the courier's tracking portal directly.

No login is required to use the SMS link.

### 3. Track via Email

A dispatch confirmation email is sent to your registered email address with the same AWB number and tracking link. Check your Promotions or Updates folder if you don't see it in your inbox.

### 4. Track Directly on the Courier's Website

If you have your AWB number, you can also track directly on the courier partner's website:

- **Delhivery**: delhivery.com → Track → Enter AWB
- **Blue Dart**: bluedart.com → Track Shipments
- **XpressBees**: xpressbees.com → Track Order
- **Ekart**: ekartlogistics.com → Track
- **DTDC**: dtdc.com → Track Shipment

### 5. Track via WhatsApp

Send your **Order ID** to our WhatsApp support number **+91-XXXXX-XXXXX** and our automated assistant will reply with live tracking status within seconds. Available 24/7.

## Understanding Tracking Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Order Confirmed | Your payment was successful and the order is being prepared. |
| Packed | Items have been picked and packed at the warehouse. |
| Dispatched | Shipment handed over to courier partner. AWB generated. |
| In Transit | Package is moving between courier hubs. |
| Out for Delivery | Package is with the local delivery executive and will arrive today. |
| Delivery Attempted | Delivery was tried but unsuccessful. A re-attempt will be made. |
| Delivered | Package delivered. Check "My Orders" to confirm. |
| RTO Initiated | Package could not be delivered and is returning to ShopEase. |

## Delivery Attempt Policy

Our courier partners make up to **3 delivery attempts** on consecutive business days. Before each attempt, you may receive a call or SMS from the delivery executive.

To ensure successful delivery:
- Keep your phone reachable on the day of delivery.
- Provide a complete address including flat/house number, floor, building name, street, landmark, and pin code.
- Ensure someone is available at the delivery address between 9 AM and 8 PM IST.

If all 3 attempts fail, the package is marked **RTO (Return to Origin)** and sent back to ShopEase. You will receive a refund (for prepaid orders) within 7–14 business days of the package reaching our warehouse.

## Order Delayed?

If your order has not moved in tracking for more than **48 hours**, or if the expected delivery date has passed, please contact our support team with your Order ID. We will raise a trace request with the courier partner and provide an update within **2 business days**.

## Pre-Shipment Delays

If your order is still in "Order Confirmed" or "Packed" status for more than **2 business days**, there may be a short delay at the warehouse due to high order volume. During sale periods, this can extend to **3–4 business days**. If you are concerned, contact support.
