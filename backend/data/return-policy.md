# ShopEase Return Policy

## Overview

At ShopEase, we want you to love every purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, we offer a **30-day return policy** from the date of delivery. Returns are free for most items and the process is fully online — no need to visit any store or drop-off point.

## Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions:

- The return request is raised within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- The item is **unused, unwashed, and undamaged**.
- All **original tags and labels** are still attached.
- The item is in its **original packaging** (poly bag, box, or garment bag).
- A valid proof of purchase (order ID) is available in your ShopEase account.

Items that **cannot be returned** include:

- Sale or clearance items marked "Final Sale".
- Innerwear, lingerie, and swimwear (for hygiene reasons).
- Customised or altered garments.
- Items damaged due to misuse, machine wash when hand-wash-only is specified, or improper storage.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log in to your ShopEase account and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
3. Click **Return Item** and select the relevant item.
4. Choose a return reason (size issue, quality concern, wrong item, etc.).
5. Upload a photo of the item in its current condition (required for quality and wrong-item claims).
6. Submit the request. You will receive a confirmation SMS and email within 30 minutes.

Our logistics partner will schedule a **doorstep pickup within 3–5 business days** after your request is approved. You do not need to visit any courier office.

## Refund Timeline

Once the returned item is received at our fulfilment centre, our quality team inspects it within **2 business days**.

- **UPI / Net Banking / Card payments**: Refund credited to the original payment source within **5–7 business days** after inspection.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) orders**: Refund transferred to your bank account via NEFT within **7–10 business days**. You will need to provide your bank account details during the return request.
- **ShopEase Wallet**: Instant credit once inspection is complete. Wallet balance can be used on your next purchase.

## Partial Returns

You can return individual items from a multi-item order. Only the price of the returned item(s) will be refunded. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to a ShopEase error (wrong or damaged item sent).

## Return Pickup Locations

We currently offer doorstep pickup across **19,000+ pin codes in India**, covering all metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad) as well as hundreds of Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns. Enter your pin code during the return request to confirm availability.
