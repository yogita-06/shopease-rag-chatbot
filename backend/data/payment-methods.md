# ShopEase Payment Methods

## Accepted Payment Options

ShopEase supports a wide range of payment methods to make checkout as convenient as possible for every customer across India.

### UPI (Unified Payments Interface)

UPI is the fastest and most popular way to pay on ShopEase. Supported apps include:

- **Google Pay (GPay)**
- **PhonePe**
- **Paytm**
- **BHIM UPI**
- **Amazon Pay**
- Any bank's UPI app (SBI Pay, iMobile Pay, Axis Pay, etc.)

Simply enter your UPI ID or scan the QR code at checkout. Payments reflect instantly with no additional charges.

### Debit and Credit Cards

We accept all major card networks:

- **Visa**
- **Mastercard**
- **RuPay** (including RuPay Platinum and Select cards)
- **American Express**

Cards are processed via a PCI-DSS-compliant secure gateway. For added security, all card transactions are protected with **3D Secure (OTP-based) authentication** from your bank.

**EMI on Credit Cards**: Purchases above ₹1,000 are eligible for No-Cost EMI on select credit cards from HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, and Kotak bank. EMI tenures available: 3, 6, 9, and 12 months. EMI options are displayed at checkout if your card is eligible.

### Net Banking

Direct bank transfer is available for all major Indian banks, including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, Canara Bank, Union Bank, and 50+ others. Select your bank at checkout and you will be redirected to your bank's secure portal to authorise the payment.

### Wallets

Pay using popular digital wallets:

- **Paytm Wallet**
- **Mobikwik**
- **Freecharge**
- **ShopEase Wallet** (store credit from refunds or rewards)

ShopEase Wallet balance never expires and can be used for partial or full payment on any order.

### Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available for orders **up to ₹5,000** at eligible pin codes. There is no additional COD handling fee. Pay in cash to the delivery executive at the time of delivery. Please keep the **exact amount ready** — our delivery partners may not carry change.

COD is not available for:
- Orders above ₹5,000.
- Pin codes not serviceable by our COD logistics partners (you will be notified at checkout).
- Flash sale and limited-time deals (prepaid only).

### Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)

ShopEase offers BNPL via **Simpl** and **LazyPay**. You can shop now and pay within 15 days or convert to a 3-month instalment plan. Eligibility is based on your credit profile and is assessed instantly at checkout.

## Payment Security

All transactions on ShopEase are secured with:

- **256-bit SSL encryption** on every page.
- **PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance** for card data handling.
- **Two-factor authentication (OTP)** for cards and net banking.
- ShopEase does **not store** your full card number or CVV.

## Failed Payments

If your payment fails but the amount is debited from your account, it will be **automatically reversed within 3–7 business days** by your bank. No action is required from your side. If the amount is not reversed after 7 business days, please contact our support team with your order ID and bank transaction reference number.

## GST and Invoices

All orders include a **GST-compliant invoice** available for download in the **My Orders** section. Business customers can add their **GSTIN** (GST Identification Number) at checkout to receive a B2B invoice.
