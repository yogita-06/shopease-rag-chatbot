# ShopEase Sizing Guide

## Why Sizing Matters

Getting the right size is the most important step to a satisfying online clothing purchase. ShopEase follows **Indian standard sizing**, which may differ from UK, US, or European sizing used by international brands. Always refer to the size chart on each product page, as measurements can vary between brands and styles.

## How to Measure Yourself

Use a soft measuring tape and measure over light-fitting clothes or directly on your body. Keep the tape snug but not tight.

- **Chest / Bust**: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
- **Waist**: Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist, typically 2–3 cm above your navel.
- **Hips**: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, usually 18–20 cm below your waist.
- **Shoulder**: Measure from the tip of one shoulder to the tip of the other across the back.
- **Inseam (for bottoms)**: Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of your leg.

## Tips for Choosing the Right Fit

1. **Between two sizes?** Size up for tops if you have a broad back or chest. For bottoms, size up if your hips or thighs are on the higher end of the range.
2. **Ethnic wear**: Kurtas and salwar-kameez sets typically have more ease (extra room) than Western tops. Refer to the specific garment measurements listed on each product page.
3. **Fabric matters**: Stretchable fabrics (lycra, spandex blends) are more forgiving. Go with your exact measurement. For rigid fabrics (cotton, denim), consider sizing up by one.
4. **Denim and trousers**: Sizes listed on the product page for denims are in Indian waist size (28, 30, 32, 34, 36, etc.). These correspond to the waist measurement in inches.

## Size Guide Per Category

- **T-shirts and shirts**: Follow the men's/women's chart based on chest and shoulder.
- **Sarees and lehengas**: One size fits most (free size blouses are available in 36–42 inch bust). Custom stitching is not available.
- **Footwear**: Listed in Indian/UK sizes. Refer to individual product pages for the specific brand's foot-length guide.
