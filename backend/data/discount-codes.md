# ShopEase Discount Codes & Offers

## How to Apply a Discount Code

1. Add items to your cart and proceed to **Checkout**.
2. On the order summary page, find the **"Apply Coupon"** field.
3. Type or paste your coupon code exactly as provided (codes are case-insensitive).
4. Click **Apply**. The discount will be deducted from your order total instantly.
5. Complete payment. The discounted amount will be shown in your order confirmation and invoice.

**Important**: Only **one coupon code** can be applied per order. Coupon codes cannot be combined with each other, but they can be used alongside bank offers (EMI discounts, cashback from your bank).

## Types of Offers

### Percentage Discount
Reduces the order total by a percentage (e.g., 10% off). A maximum cap may apply (e.g., "10% off, max ₹200").

### Flat Discount
Reduces the order total by a fixed rupee amount (e.g., ₹150 off on orders above ₹999).

### Free Shipping Coupon
Waives the ₹49 standard delivery fee. Only applicable on orders that are not already eligible for free shipping.

### Cashback Offers
Cashback is credited to your bank account or ShopEase Wallet within **5–7 business days** after order delivery. Cashback is not immediate — it is not deducted at checkout.

## Coupon Code Rules and Restrictions

- Coupons are **non-transferable** and cannot be resold.
- Coupons cannot be applied on **already discounted (sale) items** unless the offer specifically says otherwise.
- Coupons cannot be used on **gift card purchases**.
- Expired coupons cannot be reactivated. If you believe a code was valid and didn't work, contact support.
- ShopEase reserves the right to **cancel orders** placed with fraudulently obtained coupon codes.
- If a coupon was applied and you subsequently return the order, the **refund is for the amount you paid** (after coupon discount), not the original price.

## Coupon Not Working?

Common reasons a coupon code fails:

- The code has **expired**.
- Your order total is **below the minimum** required value.
- The code applies to a **specific category** that your cart doesn't include.
- The code has already been **used** (single-use codes).
- There is a **typo** in the code — double-check spacing and special characters.

If none of the above apply, contact our support team with the code and your cart details.
